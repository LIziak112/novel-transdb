Chapter 1

The Rain

It was a cold morning on the island. Eve
wrapped her scarf tighter, watching the
boats roll in with the tide.

12

She carried a small handbag and a bottle
of brandy, as she always did.

"Where is Eve?" said the captain, leaning
on the rail.

"I don't know," Eve replied, and stared
at the grey sea.

They called her Eve because she was the
first woman born on the island.
