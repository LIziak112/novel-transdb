Chapter 1

第一章

It was a cold morning on the island. Eve wrapped her scarf tighter, watching the boats roll in with the tide.

岛上的清晨很冷。夏娃把围巾裹得更紧，望着船只随潮入港。

She carried a small handbag and a bottle of brandy, as she always did.

她拎着一只手袋，还带着一瓶白兰地——她一向如此。

"Where is Eve?" said the captain, leaning on the rail.

“夏娃在哪儿？”船长倚着栏杆问。

The boats left without her that night.
